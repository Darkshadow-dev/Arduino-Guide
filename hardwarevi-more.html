LED Wiring

LED with current-limiting resistor.

Wiring Explained

Connect pin 13 to one end of the resistor.

Then connect the positive end of the LED (longer leg) to the other end of the 220Ω resistor.

Finally, connect the shorter leg of the LED to Arduino GND.
