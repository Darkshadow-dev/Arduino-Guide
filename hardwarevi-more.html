Click on the PWM LED example.

Wiring PWM LED

Wiring Explained

Connect pin 9 to one end of the resistor.

Then connect the positive end of the LED (longer leg) to the other end of the 220Ω resistor.

Finally, connect the shorter leg of the LED to Arduino GND.

Wiring LED and a Button

Click on the Button LED example.

Wiring LED + Button

Wiring Explained

Connect pin 13 to one end of the resistor.

Then connect the other side of the resistor to leg 1 on push button. And then leg 4 to positive leg of the LED.

All push buttons are 1 2 3 4 and intrnaly 1-3 2-4 are connected to get push=on do 1-4 or 2-3!

If you want push=stop do 1-3 or 2-4,

Only pushing the button will turn the LED on!

Finally, connect the shorter leg of the LED to Arduino GND.
